# Community conduct

Treat participants respectfully across experience levels and backgrounds. Critique proposals and evidence, not people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, repeated disruption and disclosure of private information are unacceptable.

The repository maintainer handles reports and may remove content or restrict participation proportionately. Use a private GitHub contact route to the maintainer for sensitive reports; do not publish another person's private information in an issue. There is no dedicated moderation team or response-time commitment. Explain moderation decisions when doing so protects the people involved, and allow a private appeal. Security findings use [Security](SECURITY.md).
